# Requirement Clarification

The questions you ask in the first 5 minutes reveal more about your engineering maturity than the class diagram you draw. This is your structured question bank.

---

## The Golden Rule

**Never assume. Clarify scope before designing.**

A parking lot that needs reservation support is architecturally different from one that doesn't. Get scope locked before spending tokens on class diagrams.

---

## Universal Questions (Ask for Any System)

### Scale & Volume

```
- How many users do we expect? (100 vs 100M changes everything)
- What's the expected request rate? (req/s, events/day)
- What's the peak-to-average ratio? (10x peak → need autoscaling design)
- How much data will we store? (affects indexing, partitioning decisions)
```

### Consistency & Availability

```
- What's the acceptable downtime? (99.9% vs 99.99%)
- Is eventual consistency acceptable for reads?
- What operations must be strongly consistent? (payments, inventory)
- What's the RPO/RTO? (recovery point / recovery time objectives)
```

### Scope Boundary

```
- Which features are in scope for this design session?
- Do I need to design auth/billing/admin or just the core domain?
- Single service or multiple services?
- New system or integrating with existing?
```

### Operations & Deployment

```
- Cloud-native or on-premise?
- Any existing tech stack constraints?
- Team size and structure? (monolith vs microservices often follows team structure)
```

## Domain-Specific Question Banks

### E-Commerce / Order Systems

```
- Can users have multiple carts (wishlists vs active cart)?
- Is inventory tracked at SKU level or variant level?
- What payment methods need support?
- Is partial order fulfillment needed?
- B2B (bulk orders, invoicing) or B2C (individual retail)?
- Is discount/coupon stacking allowed?
- Returns and refunds — in scope?
```

### Notification Systems

```
- Channels needed: email, SMS, push, WhatsApp?
- Are notifications real-time or can they be delayed?
- Can users configure notification preferences?
- Is notification deduplication needed?
- What's the delivery guarantee: at-least-once or exactly-once?
- Internationalization / multi-language support?
```

### Payment Systems

```
- Domestic only or international?
- Currencies to support?
- Which payment methods: card, UPI, net banking, wallet?
- Who is the merchant — one company or marketplace (multiple merchants)?
- Refund flows in scope?
- Dispute handling in scope?
- PCI-DSS compliance requirements?
```

### Ride-Sharing / Marketplace

```
- Supply and demand sides: who are the two parties?
- Is pricing dynamic or fixed?
- What's the matching algorithm priority: speed, price, rating?
- How do cancellations work?
- Is the platform responsible for payments or just matching?
```

### Rate Limiter

```
- Per-user, per-IP, per-API-key, or per-endpoint?
- What happens when limit exceeded: reject or queue?
- Sliding window or fixed window?
- Distributed (multiple nodes) or single node?
- Per-tenant rate limits for SaaS?
```

### Job Scheduler

```
- Cron-style (recurring) or one-time jobs?
- Distributed execution or single-node?
- Job priority levels?
- What happens to jobs missed during downtime?
- Maximum job execution time (timeout)?
- Retry on failure? How many times?
```

## Functional vs Non-Functional Requirements Template

### FRs — What the system does

Format: "The system shall allow [actor] to [action] so that [outcome]"

```
Example for Notification System:
- Users can subscribe to notification channels (email, SMS, push)
- System can send notifications triggered by domain events
- Users can set quiet hours (no notifications between 10pm-8am)
- Admins can broadcast announcements to all users
- System retries failed notifications up to 3 times
```

### NFRs — How well it does it

|Category|Questions to Answer|Example Value|
|---|---|---|
|Latency|P99 response time?|< 200ms for API, < 5s for notification delivery|
|Throughput|Requests per second?|10,000 req/s peak|
|Availability|Uptime SLA?|99.9% (8.7 hrs downtime/year)|
|Consistency|Eventual or strong?|Eventual for reads, strong for writes|
|Durability|Data loss tolerance?|Zero data loss for payments|
|Scalability|Scale to how many X?|100M users, 1M events/day|
|Security|Auth requirements?|OAuth2, RBAC, PII encryption|

## What Good Scope Bounding Sounds Like

```
Interviewer: "Design a food delivery backend."

Good engineer: 
"Before I start, let me clarify scope.
 
 Am I designing just the order flow, or also:
 - Restaurant onboarding and menu management?
 - Real-time driver tracking?
 - Payment processing?
 - Reviews and ratings?
 
 For scale, should I assume:
 - Single city or national?
 - Hundreds of orders/day or peak like Zomato (millions)?
 
 And for the LLD specifically — 
 should I focus on the core order domain 
 or also design the notification and payment systems?"
```

---

## Deriving Design Decisions from NFRs

|NFR|Design Decision|
|---|---|
|High read volume|CQRS, caching, read replicas|
|Strong consistency required|Synchronous operations, ACID transactions|
|Eventual consistency OK|Event-driven, async processing|
|99.99% availability|Active-active, circuit breakers, graceful degradation|
|Data must never be lost|Event sourcing, WAL, replication|
|Low latency|In-process operations, cache-first|
|Audit trail required|Event sourcing, immutable log|
|Multi-tenancy|Row-level security, schema-per-tenant|
