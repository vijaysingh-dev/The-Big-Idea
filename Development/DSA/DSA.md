# DSA Problem Solving Framework (For Me)

## Goal
Not:
- “Can I solve this problem?”

But:
- “Will I recognize this pattern instantly next time?”

---

# 1. Problem Attempt Strategy

## Phase 1 — Exploration (10–15 min max)

Focus on:
- Understanding constraints
- Finding brute force
- Observing patterns/signals
- Thinking about invariants

Ask:
- What changes?
- What stays fixed?
- Can I process from left/right?
- Is there ordering involved?
- Can sorting help?
- Can previous computation help?

---

## If stuck after 10–15 min

Take a SMALL hint.

Do NOT waste 1 hour stuck early.

Reason:
- I already know DSA fundamentals.
- My bottleneck is pattern recognition under uncertainty.

---

# 2. Hint Escalation Strategy

## Hint Level 1 (Preferred)
Pattern direction only.

Examples:
- Think greedy
- Scan from right
- Maintain invariant
- Use monotonic property
- Two pointers after sorting

---

## Hint Level 2
Structural insight.

Examples:
- Find pivot
- Maintain stack
- Track prefix/suffix
- Detect first violation

---

## Hint Level 3
Partial algorithm.

Use only if heavily blocked.

---

## Full Solution
Only after:
- ~30–45 min total
OR
- repeated failure after hints

---

# 3. Review Strategy (MOST IMPORTANT)

## Review 1 — Same Day (5 min)
Without code:
- Explain approach aloud
- Identify:
  - Pattern
  - Trigger
  - Optimization leap
  - Why brute force fails

---

## Review 2 — 2–3 Days Later
Redo from scratch.

Allowed:
- Read only title/notes
Not allowed:
- Reading full solution immediately

---

## Review 3 — 7 Days Later
Redo quickly (10–15 min max).

Goal:
- Instant recognition

---

## Review 4 — 21–30 Days Later
Long-term retention check.

---

# 4. Never Review By Reading Code

Bad:
- “Yeah I understand this.”

Good:
- Blank page reconstruction

If I cannot reproduce it:
- I do NOT fully know it yet.

# 6. Mental Model During Hard Problems

Normal process:

Confusion
→ Observe structure
→ Find invariant
→ Pattern emerges

Do NOT panic if pattern is not immediate.

Strong problem solvers:
- stay functional while confused.

---

# 7. Permutation Problems (Like Next Permutation)

Correct mindset:
- NOT “generate permutations”
- Think:
  “What is the SMALLEST possible change that makes this larger?”

Observe:
- Where order breaks
- What suffix is already maximized
- Minimal lexicographic modification

Key idea:
- Minimal modification
- Preserve ordering as much as possible

---

# 8. Problem Classification

Do NOT classify as:
- Easy
- Hard

Instead classify as:
- Known pattern
- Modified known pattern
- New invariant discovery

# 9. What I Should Optimize For

Less grinding.
